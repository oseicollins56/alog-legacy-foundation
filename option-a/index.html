Four promises, one legacy

Everything we do comes back to the four words on our logo.

  • Care

    Food, clothing, health checks and a safe adult who shows up. The basics come first.

  • Education

    School fees, uniforms, books and tutoring, so no child stays home because of cost.

  • Opportunity

    Skills training and apprenticeships for young people and adults ready to work.

  • For life

    We stay with families for years, not weeks, and follow each child into adulthood.

A home for every child's potential

Across Ghana, too many bright children leave school early, go to bed hungry or grow up without someone in their corner. ALOG Legacy Foundation exists to change that, one family at a time.

We work alongside parents, teachers, churches and community leaders to find the children and adults who need help most. Then we stay. A school uniform this year becomes exam fees next year, and a trade in a few years more.

  • Children first, but no one in need is turned away
  • Support delivered directly in local communities
  • Every cedi accounted for and reported back to donors
