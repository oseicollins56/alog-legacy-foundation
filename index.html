About the foundation

Welcome to ALOG Legacy Foundation

Together we can give children a brighter future

Across Ghana, too many bright children leave school early, go to bed hungry or grow up without someone in their corner. ALOG Legacy Foundation exists to change that, one family at a time.

We help people of every age with care, education and opportunity, and we always start with the children. We work alongside parents, teachers, churches and community leaders, and we stay for the long run.

What we stand for

Four promises, one legacy

  • Care

    Food, clothing, health checks and a safe adult who shows up.

  • Education

    School fees, uniforms, books and tutoring for every child.

  • Opportunity

    Skills training, sport and apprenticeships for children, youth and adults.

  • For life

    We stay with families for years and follow each child into adulthood.
